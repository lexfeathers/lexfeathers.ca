Faith Falkner approached me in 2023 to compose the score for act 1 of the Stardust audiobook, which accompanies a [tabletop game](https://sublightgames.com/game/) of the same name.

This was the first time I've been asked to score an audiobook, and I found myself fascinated by the fine line between unobtrusive and extravagant I've had to walk in putting together work we're happy with. I opted for a somber palette, making extensive use of my Starcaster played with a cello bow (wink wink) and my Osmose.

Stardust Act 1 is set to release in early 2027. For the moment, a sample from my score for the project can be heard below:
